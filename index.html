About

About Me

I am a PhD researcher in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Old Dominion University, developing expertise across power systems, electrical infrastructure, critical power, energy storage, and battery systems.

My interests span power systems, electrical distribution, protection and controls, substations, electrical equipment, power-system studies, power quality, power electronics, renewable energy, data-center power infrastructure, and energy storage.

My current research focuses on battery characterization and degradation, battery management, controlled cycling, SOC and temperature effects, transient load profiles, and experimental dataset development using battery testing infrastructure.

I am also interested in the intersection of electrical infrastructure, energy storage, critical facilities, data centers, and electrified transportation.

Projects

Engineering Projects

Selected engineering projects spanning battery systems, energy storage, electrical infrastructure, embedded sensing, and applied engineering research.

Battery Degradation & Characterization

Experimental investigation of lithium-ion battery behavior using an Arbin battery testing platform. The work focuses on controlled cycling, rest periods, SOC variation, temperature effects, transient load profiles, and battery degradation.

Areas: Battery Systems, Energy Storage, Battery Testing, Degradation, SOC/SOH

Battery Performance Dataset Development

Development of structured experimental datasets containing voltage, current, temperature, SOC, capacity, energy, cycle information, and application-oriented load profiles for battery performance and degradation analysis.

Areas: Battery Data, Experimental Design, Data Analysis, Energy Storage

Data Center Electrical Infrastructure

Developing engineering knowledge of critical electrical infrastructure supporting data centers, including utility power, MV/LV distribution, transformers, switchgear, UPS systems, generators, redundancy, and critical loads.
